# Presenton

Create presentations from a topic or your source material, review the outline, edit slides and export your work as PDF or PowerPoint.

## Create your first presentation

1. Open Presenton from Nimi Home and keep Nimi running.
2. In **Settings → AI models**, configure text generation. Generated artwork needs an image model; icon and document search also use embeddings.
3. Choose the presentation workflow, provide your topic or material and review the proposed outline before generating slides.
4. Edit text, images and layout, then export the finished presentation.

AI drafts can contain inaccurate facts and text that does not fit the layout. Check both content and exported pages before sharing. A model must support the requested structured output, tools or image inputs; an unavailable capability produces an error.

## Data and services

Saved drafts and assets belong to the App workspace. AI requests follow your selected Nimi Local or Cloud configuration; cloud requests send the relevant source content to the selected service.

Stock-image services, external search and Presenton Community are optional business services with separate settings. Community sign-in does not replace your Nimi AI configuration. Reopening a completed outline restores it; explicitly regenerating creates a new draft.

For help, report the workflow, error and export format to the App provider. Remove private source material from reports.
